Privacy Policy

Rekey · Last updated 10 November 2026

In short: Rekey has no account, no server and no internet permission. It stores no passwords. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

  • The master secret, inside your phone's secure hardware (the Android Keystore). It can't be exported, not even by the app, and it's used only after a fingerprint touch.
  • Your site list: for each entry, the site's domain, your login name, a version number, the password rules, a note if you add one, the master generation, and any apps you linked to it. None of it is a password.
  • Trusted browsers: the package name and a fingerprint of the signing certificate of each browser you chose to trust for autofill.
  • App settings: your theme choice and the last few sites you used, for the "Recent" shortcuts. These never go into an export.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage. No other app can read it, and the app turns off Android cloud backup for it.

What the app never stores

  • Passwords. Each one is recomputed when you need it and wiped from memory when you leave the screen.
  • Your 12 backup words. They're shown once at setup for you to write on paper, then wiped.
  • Your fingerprint. Android never gives apps fingerprint data; the app only learns that a touch succeeded.

Autofill

When you tap a login field, Android sends Rekey the structure of that screen: the web address the browser reports, or the name of the app, and which fields are the login and password. Rekey uses this only to find the matching entry, on the phone, at that moment. It doesn't keep it, except the app name when you choose to link an app to a site.

Clipboard

Autofill is the normal way to fill a password. If you tap Copy instead, the password is put on the clipboard marked as sensitive and cleared after 30 seconds.

Permissions

  • Biometrics: to ask for your fingerprint before a password is made.
  • That's all. There is no internet, storage, contacts or location permission. Export and import use the system file picker, and the app opens only the file you choose.

Exports

When you export, the app writes your site list (the same non-secret fields listed above) to a .json file in the place you choose. What happens to that file next is up to you.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app. Uninstalling the app removes the site list, the settings and the keys from the phone. Without your 12 words, the passwords can't be made again.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
